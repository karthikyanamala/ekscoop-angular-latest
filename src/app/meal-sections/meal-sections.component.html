🍽️ Protein-Rich Poha Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups flattened rice (poha)
  • 1 scoop YOU x 0.8 protein powder
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Lemon juice

Instructions:

  1. Rinse poha gently and drain water completely
  2. Heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds
  3. Add onions, green chilies, and peas. Sauté until soft
  4. Add turmeric powder and salt
  5. Add the drained poha and mix gently
  6. Mix YOU x 0.8 protein powder with 2 tbsp water and add to poha
  7. Cook for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally
  8. Garnish with coriander leaves and lemon juice
  9. Serve hot with your morning protein boost!

🍛 Protein-Enhanced Dal Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup yellow moong dal
  • 1 scoop YOU x 0.8 protein powder
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Instructions:

  1. Wash and cook dal with turmeric until soft
  2. Heat ghee in a pan, add cumin seeds
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste, sauté for 1 minute
  4. Add onions, cook until golden brown
  5. Add tomatoes, chili powder, and salt
  6. Cook until tomatoes are soft and mushy
  7. Add cooked dal and simmer for 10 minutes
  8. Mix YOU x 0.8 protein powder with little water and stir into dal
  9. Garnish with coriander and serve hot with rice

🥪 Protein Power Snacks Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices whole wheat bread
  • 1 scoop YOU x 0.8 protein powder
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (chopped)
  • 2 tbsp paneer, grated
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • Green chutney

Instructions:

  1. Mix protein powder with 3 tbsp water to make a paste
  2. Heat butter in a pan, add mixed vegetables
  3. Sauté vegetables until tender
  4. Add grated paneer and protein paste
  5. Season with chaat masala, pepper, and salt
  6. Cook for 2-3 minutes until well combined
  7. Spread green chutney on bread slices
  8. Add the protein-veggie mixture as filling
  9. Toast lightly and garnish with mint leaves
  10. Enjoy your protein-packed evening snack!

🍽️ Protein-Rich Roti & Curry Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 scoop YOU x 0.8 protein powder
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Instructions:

  1. Mix wheat flour with protein powder and knead into soft dough
  2. Let the dough rest for 20 minutes
  3. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and onions, sauté until golden
  5. Add tomatoes and cook until soft
  6. Add mixed vegetables and all spices
  7. Cook vegetables until tender
  8. Roll out rotis from the protein-enriched dough
  9. Cook rotis on hot tawa until puffed
  10. Serve hot rotis with the protein-packed vegetable curry
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander for the perfect dinner!
